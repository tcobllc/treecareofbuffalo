PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly.

We're a tree crew, not a data company. Here is everything this site does with your information.

This policy covers treecareofbuffalo.com, run by Tree Care of Buffalo, LLC, Buffalo, New York.

What we collect

  • What you send us. The estimate form opens an email in your own mail app addressed to business@treecareofbuffalo.com; nothing is stored on this website. Whatever you include, name, phone, address, photos, comes to us as ordinary email and is used to schedule and price your work.
  • What every web server sees. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) to keep the site running. We don't use them to identify you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics vendors, no cookies set by this site, and no third-party scripts.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, except as required to do the work you asked for (for example, a utility mark-out request includes the job address) or as required by law.

Keeping and deleting

Estimate emails and job records are kept as ordinary business records, invoices, insurance documentation, and the like. If you want your contact information deleted from our email and records, ask and we'll do it, subject to what tax and insurance rules make us keep.

Questions

Email business@treecareofbuffalo.com or call (716) 601-8275 and ask for whoever handles the paperwork.
