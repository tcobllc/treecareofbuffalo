Tree Service in Tonawanda

Brick capes, aging Norway maples, and yards where every limb rides a rope.

Tonawanda's streets, town and city both, were planted heavily with Norway maples in the mid-1900s, and a lot of them are hitting the end of the road at the same time: crown dieback up top, girdling roots below, big limbs going bare over small yards. Add the wind that comes up the Niagara, and Tonawanda is steady work for a careful crew.

What we do most here

  • Removals of declining Norway maples, taken down in pieces because there is nowhere in a Tonawanda side yard to drop anything.
  • Deadwooding the ones still worth keeping, so the lawn stops collecting branches after every front.
  • Stump grinding tight to walks and drives on compact lots.
  • Clearance work over garages and service drops, where crowns and cables have gotten too friendly.

Tight-lot rigging is the local specialty: rope on every piece, one clear lane, and the fence line, the garden beds, and the neighbor's side all untouched when the truck pulls away. Kenmore's little brother yards work the same way, and we work Kenmore too.
