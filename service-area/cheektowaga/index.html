Serving Cheektowaga

Tree Service in Cheektowaga

Postwar streets, fifty-year-old silver maples, and driveways that deserve better.

Cheektowaga's housing went up fast after the war, and so did its trees. The silver maples planted in front of all those ranches and capes in the fifties are now sixty feet tall, and silver maple is fast wood: it grows quick, it grows big, and it breaks easier than the hard maples do. Most of our Cheektowaga calls start with one of them leaning over a driveway, a garage, or the neighbor's above-ground pool.

What we do most here

  • Weight reduction on big silver maples before the wind does it less carefully.
  • Full removals on lots where the tree has simply outgrown a fifty-foot-wide parcel.
  • Stump grinding on small front lawns, where a stump eats a real fraction of the yard.
  • Storm cleanup. The flat, open stretches near the airport take wind head-on, and the brittle species show it first.

Small-lot work is rigging work. There's rarely room to drop anything free in Cheektowaga, so limbs come down on ropes, into the one clear lane we set up, and the fence, the garden, and the pool stay exactly as we found them.

From Garden Village to the Depew line, the truck is in Cheektowaga constantly; it's ten minutes from home base. The look is free, the estimate is written, and you pay when the yard is clean.
