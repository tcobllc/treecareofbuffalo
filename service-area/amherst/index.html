Tree Service in Amherst

Old oaks in Snyder, clay-soil roots up north, and the ash the borer left behind.

Amherst is really two tree towns. The older streets, Eggertsville, Snyder, the Village of Williamsville, carry mature oaks and sugar maples that have been shading those houses for a century and mostly deserve pruning, not removal. The newer subdivisions north of Maple Road sit on heavy clay, where drainage is slow, roots run shallow, and a wet fall windstorm can tip a whole spruce with the root plate attached.

What we do most here

  • Careful structural pruning on the big Snyder and Eggertsville oaks, done in the cold months to keep oak wilt off the street.
  • Dead ash removal. The emerald ash borer went through Amherst like everywhere else in Erie County, and standing dead ash along back lot lines drops limbs without warning.
  • Leaning evergreens on clay: assessed honestly. Some can be saved with weight off the sail; some are done and safer down.
  • Clearance pruning where crowns have closed over rooflines in the older neighborhoods.

The estimate is free and written whether it's a one-limb job in Williamsville or a row of dead ash behind a fence line. If a tree doesn't need to come down, we say so; there's enough real work in Amherst without inventing any.
