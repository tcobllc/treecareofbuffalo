Tree Service in Kenmore

A village grid of small yards under some of the biggest street maples in Erie County.

Kenmore packs a lot of tree into not much lot. The village's silver maples went in generations ago, and today they're seventy feet tall, standing over forty-foot-wide parcels, garages, alleys, and service drops. When one of them needs work, there is no open ground anywhere near it. That's not a complication of the job here; it is the job.

What we do most here

  • Piece-by-piece removals where every limb is roped, lowered into the one clear lane, and walked out front. Nothing free-falls in a Kenmore yard.
  • Weight and clearance pruning on the big maples over garages: less sail for the wind, daylight back over the roof, limbs off the service drop before they take it down for you.
  • Stump grinding in postage-stamp lawns, where reclaiming a stump's footprint visibly grows the yard.
  • Deadwooding, because old silver maple sheds constantly and small yards show every stick.

One practical note: anything near the electric service drop gets planned around it, and if a limb is actually on the line, that's the utility first, us second. Same-street neighbors often book together here, and it saves everyone money; one setup, several trees. The look is free, and the number is in writing before a saw starts.
