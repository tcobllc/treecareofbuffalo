SERVICE AREA

From the city line to the county line.

Buffalo and the towns around it. One crew, one truck, one written number. If your town isn't listed, call anyway: the truck goes where the trees are.

Every town in Western New York grows trees a little differently, and every yard asks for a different plan. The pages below say what we see most in each place: the old maples of the first-ring suburbs, the lake wind in the south towns, the tight side yards where a drop zone has to be measured twice. The promises do not change from town to town. The look is free, the estimate is written, the estimate is the price, and you pay when the yard is clean.

CheektowagaOlder neighborhoods, mature street trees, tight side yards. AmherstBig suburban lots and the maples that came with them. TonawandaRiver wind, snow load, and trees that have seen both. West SenecaDeep yards, old growth, and the storms off the lake. LackawannaCompact lots where every drop zone is planned. HamburgLake-effect country: storm prep before the snow. Orchard ParkWooded lots that need thinning, not clearing. KenmoreNarrow drives and canopy that meets over the street.

In Buffalo itself? The city is home base. Request a free estimate or call (716) 601-8275.
