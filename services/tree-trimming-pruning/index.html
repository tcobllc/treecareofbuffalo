Tree Trimming & Pruning

Careful cuts that keep your trees healthy, shaped, and clear of the house.

A good prune is quiet work. Deadwood comes out, the long heavy limbs over the roof lose some weight, the crown gets air and light back, and afterward the tree looks like itself, only better. A bad prune is loud: hat-racked maples, topped silver maples pushing weak shoots off every stub. We do the first kind.

What a prune actually fixes

  • Limbs on the roof, in the gutters, or rubbing siding get cut back to clear the house properly.
  • Deadwood through the crown, the stuff that ends up on your lawn after every wind, comes out before it comes down on its own.
  • Long, overextended limbs get shortened so the next ice storm has less to grab.
  • Crossing and rubbing branches get resolved before they carve wounds into each other.
  • Low growth over walks, drives, and sight lines comes up to a height you can live with.

How we cut

Every cut lands just outside the branch collar, the swollen ring where limb meets trunk, because that's the tissue that closes the wound. We don't leave stubs and we don't cut flush. And we don't top trees. Topping is the fastest way to turn a strong tree into a weak one: the regrowth is fast, badly attached, and worse than what was removed. If someone quotes you a topping job, keep your money.

When to prune

Dead limbs: anytime, the sooner the better. Structural work on most species: late fall through early spring, while the tree is dormant and the crown is easy to read. Oaks specifically get pruned in the cold months to keep oak wilt out of the neighborhood. If a limb is threatening something today, the calendar loses; we come now.

What moves the price

Crown size, how much has to move, and what the limbs hang over. A maple that needs deadwood picked out is one number; the same maple needing weight off three leaders above a garage is another. Either way the number arrives in writing after a free look, and it doesn't change. Cleanup is part of the job: limbs chipped and hauled, sawdust raked, gutters cleared of what we dropped.
