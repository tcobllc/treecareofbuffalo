Emergency Storm Work

When Buffalo weather wins, we answer the phone. Down limbs and hangers handled fast.

Buffalo breaks trees three ways: lake-effect snow that loads limbs until they let go, wind off Erie that finds every weak crotch, and ice that makes both worse. When it happens to your tree, call before it gets worse. A limb resting on a roof does more damage every hour it sits, and a hanger swinging in the crown picks where it lands without consulting anyone.

What we handle

  • Trees and limbs on houses, garages, and cars, lifted and rigged off without a second round of damage.
  • Hangers: the half-broken limbs still caught in the crown. These are the ones that hurt people, and they come out first.
  • Blocked driveways and walks, cut and cleared so you can get out.
  • Split leaders and storm-torn crowns, either cabled and cleaned up or, when the tree is done, taken down before the next front finishes the job.

The one hard rule

If a line is down, or a limb is in the service drop, that's not a tree job yet. That's NYSEG's job first. Stay away from it, call the utility, and call us second: we work the tree the moment the line is dead or cleared. No tree is worth anyone's contact voltage, and a crew that says otherwise is a crew to hang up on.

Your insurance company

Storm damage on structures usually turns into a claim. We photograph the scene before we touch it, keep the cuts clean, and give you a written invoice that says what was done and why. Adjusters like paper; you'll have it.

What "fast" honestly means

After a real storm, every tree crew in Erie County is buried, and anyone promising instant service to everyone is lying to someone. We triage: trees on structures and blocked drives first, hangers second, cleanup third. Call, tell us what you've got, and you get an honest slot, not a guess. Storm calls are answered anytime, including well outside the Mon to Fri 7 to 7 the rest of the business keeps.

Not an emergency, just ugly? The broken stuff still up in the crown after everyone's power is back deserves a look before the next storm. That's a normal free estimate, and a calmer week to book it in.
